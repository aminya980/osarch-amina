**only to check compatibility (socket, memory type, M.2) and estimated power**.
Prices are in [../budget.csv](../budget.csv) from Kazakhstan vendors.

Monitors, UPS, webcam, headset and keyboard are not checked here (no
compatibility issue — USB / HDMI / DisplayPort).
